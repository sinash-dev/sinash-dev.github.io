Coinsay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Coinsay app ("the app") uses and why.

Who is responsible: Sina Shafaei. Contact: sina.shafai@gmail.com

Summary

  • Coinsay has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your settings and flip statistics stay on your device.
  • The free version shows a small banner ad provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Coinsay Pro (one-time purchase) removes the ads. Payment is processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your theme, sound and vibration settings, chosen coin and flip statistics on your device only. This data is never sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Advertising (free version)

The free version uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate location,
  • information about ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped),
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data.

Your choice (EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ads are shown, the app displays Google's consent form. You can accept, refuse, or choose individual purposes. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising and storing information on your device; our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you buy Coinsay Pro, the ad SDK no longer requests ads.

In-app purchase (Coinsay Pro)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes your payment and tells the app only whether this Google account owns Coinsay Pro, so the app can unlock it. We do not receive your name, email address or payment information. See the Google Play terms and Google's privacy policy.

Children

Coinsay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app itself does not collect personal data, most requests concern data held by Google; you can manage it at https://myaccount.google.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
